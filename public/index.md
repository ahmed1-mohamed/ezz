# منصة منارة العز التعليمية | Manarat Al-Ezz Educational Platform

> **Manarat Al-Ezz** is an integrated educational management platform for Quran recitation, Arabic language, and Islamic studies — serving students, parents, and teachers with efficiency and quality.

---

## About the Platform

**Manarat Al-Ezz** (منارة العز) provides a comprehensive digital environment for Islamic education:

- **Quran Recitation (Tajweed)** — Structured memorisation and Tajweed programmes
- **Arabic Language** — Grammar, reading, and writing curricula
- **Islamic Studies** — Fiqh, Seerah, and Aqeedah modules
- **Student Management** — Enrolment, attendance, progress tracking
- **Parent Portal** — Real-time updates on student performance
- **Teacher Dashboard** — Class scheduling, grading, and communication tools
